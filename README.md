# Credit Card Financial Dashboard — Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze credit card financial performance, customer behavior, transaction activity, revenue trends, and key business indicators.

The main objective is to help stakeholders monitor credit card operations, identify business trends, understand customer segments, and support data-driven decision-making through clear and interactive business intelligence reporting.

---

## Business Objectives

This dashboard was designed to answer key business questions:

- What is the total revenue generated by credit card operations?
- How does revenue change across weeks and quarters?
- Which card categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
- How do transaction methods such as swipe, chip, and online compare?
- Which states, age groups, income groups, and job categories contribute most to performance?
- What are the key weekly changes in revenue and transaction activity?

---

## Dataset

The project uses credit card customer and transaction data.

Main data categories include:

- Customer demographic information
- Credit card transaction records
- Card category information
- Revenue and interest-related financial measures
- Weekly performance indicators
- Customer segmentation variables

The data was prepared and analyzed to support business intelligence reporting in Power BI.

## Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several important business insights:

- Overall revenue reached approximately **57M**.
- Total interest earned reached approximately **8M**.
- Total transaction amount reached approximately **46M**.
- Blue cards generated the highest revenue among all card categories.
- Male customers contributed more revenue than female customers.
- Revenue performance can be monitored weekly through KPI cards and trend visuals.
- Customer segmentation helps identify high-value customer groups.
- Transaction method analysis helps compare swipe, chip, and online behavior.
- State-level analysis helps identify the strongest geographic markets.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customer segments.
- Monitor weekly revenue changes to detect performance shifts early.
- Use customer segmentation to improve targeting strategies.
- Track card category performance to identify strong and weak product areas.
- Analyze transaction method trends to support digital payment strategy.
- Continue updating the dashboard with new data for ongoing business monitoring.
